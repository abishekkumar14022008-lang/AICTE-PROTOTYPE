About

A Smart India Hackathon prototype for the problem statement: Integrated Dashboard for Monitoring and Tracking Institution-Related Complaint Cases Across AICTE Bureaus/Cells.

This prototype is not affiliated with or officially connected to AICTE. Authentication, multi-user access, and email/SMS alerts are planned future enhancements and are not implemented.
